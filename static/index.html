Answer bank

    
Your answers to application questions. The apply window fills one in when a form asks the same question (or one worded almost the same) and outlines it amber so you check it. Answers you type into custom questions are added here; so are your cover letters' short answers, as drafts. Contact details, dates and voluntary disclosures are never stored.
